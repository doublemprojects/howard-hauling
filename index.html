FAQ

Quick answers before pickup.

Still unsure? Text a photo and your location. That’s usually the fastest way to get an answer.

How do I get a quote?

Text 2–3 clear photos of what you want removed along with your pickup location to (240) 389-2534. We’ll review the job and confirm pricing before work begins.

Do pickups really start at $99?

Yes, qualifying pickups start at $99. The final price depends on the amount and type of material, weight, labor, access, location and disposal requirements.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Howard County, including Columbia, Ellicott City, Elkridge, Jessup, Savage, Clarksville and Fulton, plus Laurel and other nearby areas when scheduling and distance allow.

What items do you take?

Common pickups include furniture, mattresses, accepted appliances, household clutter, garage and basement junk, move-out items, cleanout debris and accepted yard debris. Text a photo if you are unsure about a specific item.

Do you donate or recycle?

Yes. Usable items are donated or recycled whenever practical.

Do I need to be available during set business hours?

We schedule pickups directly with customers rather than advertising fixed public hours. Contact us and we’ll work out an available pickup time.
